# Roadmap

Three horizons (Master PRD/TRD):

- **Horizon 1 — Postpartum continuity** (this repo, v0/v1). The six weeks
  after discharge, where recommended follow-up doesn't happen because
  nobody holds the list.
- **Horizon 2 — The record that does not close.** Same structured record
  drives follow-up for years: glucose after GDM, BP after a hypertensive
  pregnancy, overdue cervical screening. No new data collection required.
- **Horizon 3 — Women's health infrastructure.** A continuous longitudinal
  record for most adult women, starting in their twenties, as the
  substrate for NCD care and screening programmes downstream.

## Release phases

- **v0 (5 Oct – 8 Nov)**: this repo's current scope. One card format, one
  discharge-summary format, two languages, J1/J2/J3/J6 only.
- **v1 (post-finale, 3–6 months)**: J4/J5, 5–6 languages, FHIR export,
  named clinical partner site + baseline (gate: do not start without both).
- **v2 (scale)**: multi-state, ABHA/ABDM linkage, programme dashboards,
  integration path with state platforms (e.g. CHIP) rather than
  competition with them.

## Deliberately deferred, possibly forever

Baby tracking, provider marketplace, peer forums, journaling, diet content
libraries, teleconsultation, wearables — each defensible alone, each pulls
the product toward wellness and away from operations. Roadmap slide
material, not backlog.

**Scope test for any proposed feature:** does this change whether a due
care event actually happens? If no, it's roadmap regardless of how well it
would demo.
